# AssetGuru Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 - Core Brand and Digital Design System**

## 1. Brand Overview

### Brand name

**AssetGuru**

AssetGuru is a specialist multi-vendor marketplace for GameGuru MAX assets. It gives creators a polished platform through which they can discover, purchase, sell and manage game-ready content.

### Core proposition

> A premium marketplace built specifically for GameGuru MAX creators.

AssetGuru should feel more curated, trustworthy and professionally presented than a traditional community download site, while remaining approachable to independent developers and first-time asset sellers.

### Primary tagline

**Build Worlds. Inspire Players.**

### Supporting lines

- Assets made for GameGuru MAX.
- Everything you need to build your next world.
- Discover. Create. Deploy.
- From creators, for creators.
- Turn ideas into playable worlds.

### Brand promise

AssetGuru helps developers spend less time searching for compatible content and more time building games.

### Brand personality

AssetGuru is futuristic but not confusing; premium but not corporate or sterile; technical but still approachable; playful without becoming childish; energetic without overwhelming the interface; community-focused without looking amateur; and confident without making exaggerated claims.

## 2. Brand Positioning

AssetGuru sits between a professional software platform and an immersive game interface.

It should not resemble a generic ecommerce template with neon colours added afterward. The marketplace itself should feel like a creative tool: responsive, animated, rewarding to explore and unmistakably connected to game development.

### Primary audiences

**Buyers** - GameGuru MAX developers looking for environments, level kits, characters, animations, weapons, vehicles, props, textures, materials, audio, music, scripts, gameplay systems, interface assets and complete themed bundles.

**Vendors** - Independent creators and studios looking for a professional storefront, simple upload tools, analytics, pricing controls, reviews, payouts, bundles and exposure.

**Community members** - Users participating through reviews, favourites, collections, creator follows, showcases, tutorials, resources and free assets.

## 3. Brand Architecture

- **AssetGuru Marketplace** - Public shopping and discovery.
- **AssetGuru Creator Hub** - Vendor dashboard and asset administration.
- **AssetGuru Library** - Purchases, downloads, licences and updates.
- **Guru Assist** - Interactive assistant and mascot system.
- **AssetGuru Community** - Showcases, discussions, profiles and learning resources.

These should remain parts of AssetGuru rather than becoming visually separate sub-brands.

## 4. Logo System

### Primary wordmark

The primary wordmark uses **Asset** in white or soft white and **Guru** in electric cyan. It should use clean geometric lettering, tight but readable spacing and a subtle futuristic character without an excessively stylised sci-fi font.

### Brand symbol

The standalone symbol is an angular letter **A**, constructed from overlapping geometric forms. It should have a strong triangular silhouette, cyan-to-violet gradient, slight three-dimensional depth, carefully controlled glow, clear recognition at small sizes and no extremely thin details.

Use it for the favicon, app icon, loading animation, social avatar, sidebar branding, watermark, verification badge and platform launcher icon.

### Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space around the wordmark: at least the height of the letter G.
- Clear space around the symbol: approximately 20% of its width.
- Full wordmark minimum: 120 px wide digitally.
- Standalone symbol minimum: 24 x 24 px.
- Recommended favicon master: 64 x 64 px.

At very small sizes, remove glow and dimensional effects.

### Logo backgrounds

Preferred backgrounds are Void Black, Midnight Navy, deep navy gradients and dark imagery with sufficient empty space. A flat dark logo variant may be used on white or very light backgrounds.

Do not stretch, compress, rotate, recolour arbitrarily, surround with a heavy container, replace the approved typography with a novelty font or animate the logo continuously during normal browsing.

## 6. Typography

### Recommended families

- **Display and headings:** Space Grotesk, weights 500-700.
- **Interface and body:** Inter, weights 400-600.
- **Data and technical information:** JetBrains Mono, used sparingly.

### Type scale

| Style | Desktop size | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Display XL | 64-72 px | 700 |
| Display | 48-56 px | 700 |
| Page title | 36-42 px | 700 |
| Section title | 22-28 px | 600 |
| Card title | 16-18 px | 600 |
| Body large | 17-18 px | 400 |
| Body | 14-16 px | 400 |
| Small UI | 12-13 px | 500 |
| Micro label | 10-11 px | 600 |

Large promotional headings may use a controlled gradient on one important phrase. Avoid gradients on paragraphs or every heading.

Use uppercase for compact dashboard section labels, status badges, navigation groups and promotional labels. Use title case or sentence case for product names, buttons, forms, help messages and descriptions.

## 7. Layout and Spatial System

Use an 8 px spacing system, with common increments of 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64-96 px.

Recommended desktop marketplace structure:

- 80-96 px left navigation rail.
- Flexible central content area.
- Optional 300-360 px promotional sidebar.
- Maximum content width around 1,600 px.
- 24-32 px outer margins.

Use a 12-column responsive grid. Product cards should generally display five across on very wide screens, four on standard desktop, three on compact desktop, two on tablet and one on narrow mobile.

Suggested radii: 6-8 px for chips, 8-10 px for inputs and buttons, 10-12 px for product cards, 12-16 px for dashboard panels and 16-20 px for large hero panels.

## 8. Surface and Panel Style

Glass panels should use a dark navy background at 88-96% opacity, subtle background blur, a one-pixel blue-grey border, faint internal top highlight and soft shadow. Cyan or magenta edge glow is reserved for active states.

A standard asset card contains a preview image, status badge where relevant, favourite control, asset title, category, vendor, verification badge, rating, review count, price and optional cart action.

On hover, a card rises 2-4 px, the border brightens, the thumbnail scales by about 2%, a faint cyan-to-violet glow appears and quick actions fade into view within 160-220 ms.

## 9. Buttons and Controls

Use Guru Cyan for normal primary marketplace actions such as Explore Marketplace, Search, Add to Cart, Download, Save Changes and Continue. Use dark text, moderate hover glow, a strong keyboard focus ring and a minimum height of 44 px.

Use the violet-magenta gradient for promotional actions such as Join Now, Upload New Asset, View Deal, Launch Promotion and Upgrade Vendor Plan.

Secondary actions use a dark transparent background, Interface Border outline and Stellar White text, moving to cyan on hover.

Destructive actions use Error Red and require confirmation when irreversible.

Icon-only controls require a tooltip, visible hover state, keyboard focus, accessible label and at least a 40 x 40 px interactive area.

## 10. Iconography

Icons should be geometric, primarily outline-based, consistent in stroke weight, slightly rounded, readable at 16-24 px and simple enough for dense dashboards.

Filled icons are reserved for selected navigation, confirmed favourites, active cart states, alerts and major category illustrations.

## 11. Imagery and Asset Presentation

Preferred imagery includes atmospheric game environments, clear asset renders, meaningful character poses, vehicles in context, clean weapon presentations, before-and-after scene construction, texture previews, interface demonstrations and multiple angles where quality matters.

Main thumbnails should use a consistent 16:9 ratio. Keep a clear focal point and avoid hiding the asset behind heavy text.

Permitted overlays include New, Bestseller, Featured, Top Rated, sale percentage, GameGuru MAX compatibility, 4K, PBR, Audio Preview and Demo Available. Do not place more than two badges over one thumbnail.

Hero images may be cinematic and atmospheric. The surrounding interface carries the cyberpunk brand, while individual asset artwork should accurately represent its own genre.

## 12. Guru Assist Mascot

### Name and role

**Guru** is AssetGuru's interactive platform companion, providing help, recommendations, onboarding and contextual guidance.

### Visual design

Guru is an original robotic feline or small futuristic companion with large expressive digital eyes, a compact mechanical body, cyan holographic illumination, violet secondary lights, smooth armour panels, articulated ears and a circular holographic platform.

### Personality

Helpful, knowledgeable, curious, encouraging, brief and slightly playful - never patronising.

### Behaviour

Guru may greet returning users occasionally, explain controls, suggest filters, surface missing listing information, highlight vendor activity, guide uploads, help locate purchases and explain compatibility or licences.

Guru must not cover important controls, interrupt checkout, repeat dismissed suggestions, create constant motion, pressure purchases, imitate a human support agent or imply actions were completed when they were not.

Display modes: Docked, Prompt, Expanded, Quiet and Disabled.

## 13. Animation and Motion

Motion should communicate state or hierarchy, keep content readable, avoid being essential to comprehension, keep continuous effects subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Recommended motion includes soft logo illumination while loading, slow particles, brief border pulses, search focus travel, card lift, upload progress, graphs drawing into view, category icon response and gentle Guru idle animation.

| Interaction | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Button hover | 120-160 ms |
| Card hover | 160-220 ms |
| Tooltip | 150-200 ms |
| Panel transition | 220-300 ms |
| Page transition | 280-400 ms |
| Guru entrance | 350-500 ms |
| Atmospheric loop | 8-20 seconds |

Avoid constant flashing, rapid neon flicker, large parallax movement, long transitions, excessive cursor-following and animating every card at once.

## 14. Voice and Tone

AssetGuru speaks like an experienced creator helping another creator: direct, clear, knowledgeable, positive, practical and creator-focused.

Good marketplace copy: "Build your next environment with production-ready assets from verified creators."

Good vendor guidance: "Add at least three preview images to help buyers understand what is included."

Errors should explain what happened, whether anything was saved and what the user should do next.

Empty states should feel helpful rather than unfinished.

## 15. Marketplace Labels and Badges

Approved product badges: New, Featured, Bestseller, Top Rated, Staff Pick, Updated, Free, Sale, GameGuru MAX, PBR, 4K, Audio Preview and Demo Available.

Vendor badges:

- **Verified Creator** - Identity or seller information confirmed.
- **Pro Seller** - Meets defined commercial or quality criteria.
- **Top Seller** - Awarded through transparent performance criteria.
- **Community Creator** - Trusted contributor of free or educational assets.

Badges must have documented requirements and should not be sold without clear disclosure.

## 16. Accessibility

Maintain strong contrast, use more than glow to define boundaries, provide non-glowing focus outlines, maintain 44 x 44 px touch targets where practical, support keyboard navigation, add useful alternative text, never rely only on colour for status, support browser zoom, respect reduced motion, allow Guru animations to be paused, avoid flashing, caption speech in videos and supplement graphs with text summaries.

## 17. Responsive Behaviour

**Desktop:** Persistent sidebar, hero art, multi-column grids, promotional rail, expanded Guru and detailed analytics.

**Tablet:** Collapsible sidebar, two or three product columns, promotions moved into the content flow, stacked dashboard panels and compact Guru.

**Mobile:** Compact or bottom navigation, one-column primary content, horizontal categories, sticky search/filter access, full-screen filter drawer, simplified card actions, compact Guru and accessible chart summaries.

The mobile experience should not reproduce the desktop dashboard at a smaller scale.

## 18. Interface Do and Don't

### Do

- Use dark navy surfaces with clear depth.
- Reserve neon colours for interaction and emphasis.
- Keep product imagery prominent.
- Use animation to communicate state.
- Give dashboards strong hierarchy.
- Keep Guru's help brief and contextual.
- Make compatibility obvious.
- Show vendor identity and trust signals clearly.
- Use consistent card ratios and metadata positions.

### Don't

- Cover every border in neon.
- Use cyberpunk fonts for body text.
- Make all buttons magenta.
- Hide navigation behind effects.
- Overload cards with badges.
- Let background art reduce readability.
- Use tiny interface labels.
- Animate every object.
- Let Guru interrupt important tasks.
- Make non-cyberpunk assets feel out of place.

## 19. Design Principles

1. **The assets are the heroes.** The interface supports the content rather than competing with it.
2. **Futuristic but functional.** Advanced, but immediately understandable.
3. **Dense, not cluttered.** Rich information without visual chaos.
4. **Motion with purpose.** Animation communicates interaction, progress, context or personality.
5. **Built for creators.** Language, tools and workflow reflect how independent game developers work.

## 22. Final Brand Statement

AssetGuru is a creative marketplace that feels like part of the game-development experience itself.

Its dark atmospheric foundations, precise neon accents, cinematic imagery and intelligent companion create a distinctive environment without sacrificing clarity or usability. Buyers should feel inspired to explore. Vendors should feel that their work is being presented professionally.

**Build Worlds. Inspire Players.**
